The Backrooms: Corbenic

The next episode of No Wind Resistance

Trigger warning: death, being trapped forever, abandonment, a friend left behind on purpose, body horror, a stolen life in the Frontrooms, adult language, and a lead who gets to the bottom rung of a ladder for the second time in her life and makes the same choice.

COLD OPEN

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC (SCP-2922) - CONTINUOUS

Grey sand. Black rocks. Petrified trees with no leaves. A sky the color of a dead TV.

And in it, impossibly huge, cold, absolute: THREE GLOWING MOONS.

The crater smokes. Around it, the ash of a hundred STRIDERS drifts like snow.

MAE stands in the middle of it in her black Grail Knight armor, the Scranton Anchor glowing in her chest like an iron heart, the Firesalt broadsword cooling from white to orange in her gauntlet. Her visor is up. Her face is wet with something that might be sweat.

KEVIN, 19, sits on the roof of a rusted 2008 Honda Civic, knees pulled up, holding a tire iron. Torn graphic tee. No shoes. He's shaking.

KEVIN
You came. You actually... you blew yourself up and came.

MAE
Don't make it a thing.

KEVIN
You blew yourself up, Mae.

MAE
(Sheathing the sword)
I said don't make it a thing. Get in the car.

Kevin slides down off the roof. He opens the driver's door. The hinge screams.

KEVIN
Where are we going?

MAE
Out.

She looks up at the three moons. Something in her face doesn't like them.

MAE
Drive toward the middle one.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

INT. THE CIVIC - MOVING - LATER

The Civic bounces across the grey sand. One headlight works. The radio is on and playing only static.

Kevin drives. Mae rides shotgun, armor scraping the ceiling, the sword between her knees, point down in the footwell.

The middle moon hasn't gotten any closer.

KEVIN
How long have I been here?

MAE
How long does it feel like?

KEVIN
A day? A year? I don't know. I don't sleep. I'm not hungry. I found the car like this. Keys in it. My car. My actual car, Mae. With the Wendy's bag still in the back from before I noclipped.

He laughs. It isn't a good laugh.

KEVIN
I thought I made it. Out. I was in the Denny's. You were there, and Leo, and you said you hated Denny's, and I ordered Dasani. And then the walls started flaking.

MAE
That wasn't the Denny's.

KEVIN
What?

MAE
That was a simulation. A Foundation thing. They sedate you and play you a happy ending so you don't scream while they do the paperwork.

KEVIN
Then where were you?

Mae doesn't answer. She stares out the window.

KEVIN
Mae. Where were you, while I was in the fake Denny's?

MAE
(Quiet)
Columbus.

ACT TWO

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC - THE SAME PETRIFIED TREE - LATER

The Civic rolls to a stop.

They're beside a petrified tree. Twisted, black, with a branch shaped like a hand held out, open.

Kevin stares at it.

KEVIN
We passed this. We passed this an hour ago.

MAE
Trees look the same.

KEVIN
It's got my Doritos bag on it.

He points. Hanging from the hand-shaped branch is a crumpled bag of Cool Ranch Doritos.

KEVIN
I threw that out the window an hour ago. We've been driving straight, Mae. I didn't turn once.

Mae gets out. Armor clanking. She walks to the tree. She looks at the bag.

She looks at the three moons. They haven't moved. Not an inch.

MAE
(Under her breath)
Shit.

KEVIN (O.S.)
What? What does shit mean? Mae?

INT. THE CIVIC - CONTINUOUS

Mae gets back in. Slams the door.

MAE
Drive the other way.

KEVIN
Why?

MAE
Just do it.

INT. THE CIVIC - MOVING - LATER

They drive the other way.

The static on the radio crackles. For a second, under it, a voice. Faint. A heart monitor. BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.

Then static.

The Civic crests a dune.

Ahead: a petrified tree with a branch like an open hand. A Doritos bag.

Kevin hits the brakes.

KEVIN
No. No, no, no.

He floors it. Hard left. Into the dunes.

INT. THE CIVIC - MOVING - LATER

The tree.

INT. THE CIVIC - MOVING - LATER

The tree.

INT. THE CIVIC - MOVING - LATER

The tree.

Kevin is screaming now. Wordless. Pounding the wheel.

Mae puts her gauntlet on his hand. Very gently, for a hand made of steel.

MAE
Stop the car, Kevin.

ACT THREE

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC - THE TREE - NIGHT

It's always night here. But the moons are lower now. Watching.

Kevin sits on the hood of the Civic. Mae stands in front of him.

Something walks out of the dunes.

It's a KNIGHT. Not like Mae. Old. Rusted silver armor, dented, green with age, a tattered surcoat with three white circles on it. Three moons. It walks slowly, dragging a sword so worn it's almost a stick.

Mae draws her blade. Steps between it and Kevin.

The Knight stops ten feet away. Lifts its visor.

Inside: nothing. Grey sand, pouring slowly out of the helmet like an hourglass.

THE KNIGHT
(A voice like wind through a keyhole)
Put it away, sister. I'm not here to fight. Nobody fights here. That's what the Striders are for.

MAE
Who are you?

THE KNIGHT
A Grail Knight. Of the Three Moons. Same as you, now, by the look of that armor. We came here looking for the Cup, a long time ago. A thousand of us. The Cup gives life back. That's the story.

KEVIN
Did you find it?

THE KNIGHT
(Sand pours out of its helmet as it tilts its head)
No.

Silence.

THE KNIGHT
Corbenic is where the dead go when they died somewhere they shouldn't have. In-between. On a train going too deep. Under a reality anchor. In a wall. It's the bottom of everything, under the Backrooms, under the Hub, under the End.

It looks at Kevin.

THE KNIGHT
You died on the train, boy.

KEVIN
No I didn't. I'm right here. I'm talking.

THE KNIGHT
So am I.

Kevin looks at the sand pouring out of the helmet.

KEVIN
(Small)
No.

THE KNIGHT
Your heart stopped in the tunnel of light. The Metro went too deep and your heart couldn't come with it. Your body's in a hospital in Ohio, with a beeping machine, and the beeping stopped a long time ago. You hear it on the radio sometimes. Everyone does. It's the last thing you heard.

Kevin looks at the Civic radio. Through the open window: static. And under it, very faint, a long, flat, unbroken tone.

BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP.

KEVIN
(Shaking)
But the Denny's. Mae was in the Denny's. Leo was in the Denny's.

THE KNIGHT
The Denny's was a gift. Somebody wanted you quiet. Corbenic doesn't care. It takes what's dead. You're dead. That's all.

MAE
(Stepping forward)
Then how do I get him out?

The Knight turns its empty helmet toward her.

THE KNIGHT
You don't, sister. That's the rule. The dead have no weapons. The dead have no exits. The roads all go back to the tree. The moons never get closer. You can drive until the car rusts through and you'll still be here. I've walked for six hundred years. I've seen that tree eleven million times.

MAE
I got in.

THE KNIGHT
You're not dead. Not all the way. That thing in your chest is holding a little of you in the world. Enough. You came in through a hole you tore with it. You can go out the same way. Once. Before it burns out.

MAE
And him?

THE KNIGHT
The hole fits one.

A long silence.

The three moons hang over them.

KEVIN
(Barely a whisper)
Mae.

ACT FOUR

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC - THE TREE - LATER

The Knight is gone. Back into the dunes. Dragging its sword.

Mae and Kevin sit side by side on the hood of the Civic. Not touching.

KEVIN
You said Columbus.

MAE
Yeah.

KEVIN
What's in Columbus?

MAE
An apartment. Summit Street. Third floor. Leo's there. He's doing his GED. He drinks Mountain Dew. He plays Mario Kart.

She stares at the moons.

MAE
With you.

Kevin turns and looks at her.

KEVIN
What?

MAE
There's a you there. In Columbus. He comes over on Sundays. He's nineteen and he's in community college for something with computers and he wears a graphic tee every day, and he lets Leo win at Mario Kart, badly.

KEVIN
That's not me.

MAE
I know.

KEVIN
That's not me, Mae. I'm here. I'm dead. I'm sitting on my car in hell.

MAE
(Very quiet)
I know.

KEVIN
Then who is it?

MAE
I don't know. A Skin-Stealer. A Foundation copy. Something that came back up through the wall wearing you. I noticed in the first week. He holds the controller wrong. He doesn't like Doritos.

She laughs. Bitter.

MAE
I noticed everything, Kevin. I always notice everything.

KEVIN
And you let him come over.

MAE
Leo likes him.

KEVIN
(Standing up, furious, crying)
You let a thing with my face sit on your couch every Sunday and play Mario Kart with your brother, and you knew, and you didn't say anything, and you didn't come for me—

MAE
I CAME FOR YOU!

She's standing too. The Anchor in her chest flares blue.

MAE
I blew myself up! I dove into a reactor! I put a knife in my own spine! I'm here!

KEVIN
After how long? How many Sundays, Mae?

Mae doesn't answer.

Because the answer is a lot.

ACT FIVE

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC - THE TREE - DAWN THAT ISN'T DAWN

The moons have shifted. Only slightly. They've lined up, one behind the next.

The Anchor in Mae's chest is flickering. Blue. Dark. Blue. Dark. Like a phone at one percent.

In the sky, the rift she came through is still open. A jagged slash of VHS static and neon-blue lightning. Smaller now. Closing. Slowly. Like a wound healing.

And from the rift, down to the grey sand, hangs a LADDER.

Not a real ladder. A ladder made of light. Rungs of static, rails of blue Hume lightning. Exactly as wide as one person.

Mae and Kevin stand at the bottom of it.

KEVIN
We can both try. We can try, Mae. I'll go first, and you push, and—

MAE
It fits one. You heard it.

KEVIN
It said that. It's a bag of sand. It could be wrong.

Mae puts her gauntlet on the bottom rung. It hums. The static crawls up her arm, into the armor, toward the Anchor. Recognizing her.

She looks at Kevin.

His face. Nineteen. Messy hair. Awkward shoulders. Graphic tee. He looks like Leo would have looked if Leo had gotten to grow up somewhere with sunlight.

He looks like a kid on a ladder in Level 3.

KEVIN
Please.

INSERT: A hand slipping out of another hand. A skateboard on a pipe. Black Converse with a frog doodle on the toe, going up. Rung. Rung. Rung.

Mae closes her eyes.

MAE
(Not to Kevin. To no one.)
Leo's waiting. He's alone in that apartment with the thing that's wearing you. I left half a jar in the freezer. If I don't go back—

KEVIN
Take me with you!

MAE
I can't.

KEVIN
You can! Hold my hand and climb! You're a Grail Knight! You're a fucking reality engine! You can do anything!

MAE
I can do one thing.

She opens her eyes. They are dry.

MAE
I can climb.

Kevin grabs her arm. Both hands. Holds on.

KEVIN
Mae. Mae. Don't do this. Don't do the ladder. You told me about the ladder. You told me in Level 2 with the pipes, when you were crying, you said you'd never—

She looks down at his hands on her armor.

Then she looks past him. At the dunes. At a hundred shapes rising out of the grey sand. Twenty feet tall. Black bone. Spears.

The STRIDERS. Coming back. They always come back.

She looks at the rift. Closing.

Then she looks at Kevin.

And we see her face do the math.

It doesn't take long. It never takes long.

MAE
(Gently. So gently.)
Spatial shift.

She pulls her arm free. Steel slides out of his grip like it's oiled.

She climbs.

KEVIN
MAE!

Rung. Rung. Rung.

KEVIN
MAE, PLEASE!

She doesn't look down.

The Striders reach the tree. Spears lowered.

Kevin grabs the bottom rung of the ladder.

His hand goes right through it.

It's not for him. It never was. The dead have no exits.

He grabs again. Through. Again. Through.

Above, Mae reaches the rift. Silhouetted in blue static. For one frame, she stops. Hangs there. Her hand drifts down, open, toward him. The way it did once, a long time ago, on a ladder in Level 3.

Her fingers don't reach. They were never going to.

She goes through.

The rift closes like a mouth.

The ladder winks out.

SILENCE.

ACT SIX

EXT. THE BOG OF CORBENIC - THE TREE - LATER

The Striders surround the Civic. A hundred of them. Spears raised.

They don't strike.

They just stand there. Waiting. Clicking softly.

Kevin sits on the hood of his car. Tire iron in his lap. Not crying anymore.

He knows what they're waiting for. The Knight told him. Nobody fights here. Nobody wins. Nobody leaves. They'll stand here, and he'll sit here, and the moons will hang there, and the radio will play a flat tone, and it will go on until the stars stop.

He's fully trapped.

He looks at the Doritos bag on the hand-shaped branch. Reaches up. Takes it down. Looks inside.

There's one chip left.

He eats it. It tastes like nothing.

He leans back on the windshield, under the three moons.

The radio crackles.

Through the static, very faint, from very far away, from up past the Backrooms and the Hub and the walls, from a third-floor apartment on Summit Street, at 3:04 in the morning:

MAE (V.O.)
(Through static, a whisper)
Spatial shift. Level 3. We got separated.

Kevin closes his eyes.

KEVIN
(To the moons)
Yeah. Yeah, we did.

The Striders click.

The tone goes on.

INT. MAE'S APARTMENT - COLUMBUS - SUNDAY

Real sunlight through the gaps in the blinds.

LEO sits on the couch with a Switch controller. Happy. Soft. Eyes a little blue at the edges.

The door opens.

KEVIN walks in. Graphic tee. Messy hair. A two-liter of Mountain Dew.

He holds the controller wrong.

LEO
Kevin! You came!

KEVIN
(Smiling. Too wide. Just slightly too wide.)
Wouldn't miss it.

In the kitchen doorway, MAE stands with a mug of chamomile tea in her hands, wearing a hoodie, a scar of neon-blue burn running up her neck from under the collar where the Anchor used to be.

She watches the thing with Kevin's face sit down on her couch.

She doesn't say anything.

She never will.

KEVIN
(Without looking at her)
You hate Denny's, right, Mae?

Mae's hands tighten on the mug.

MAE
...Yeah.

KEVIN
(Grinning at the TV)
Me too.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE
