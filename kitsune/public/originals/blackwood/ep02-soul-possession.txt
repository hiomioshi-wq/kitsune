BARTENDER

Wiring’s fucked.

Sasha laughs.

Lux’s glare shifts toward her.

For a moment, the tension could cut steel.

Then Lux smiles.

LUX

Next year, Blackwood hosts.

SASHA

Next year, I’m bringing a competent roster.

VELVETTE

Where will you find one?

Sasha thinks.

SASHA

I’ll steal Blackwood’s.

LUX

Try it.

SASHA

I might.

VELVETTE

Try stealing me again and I’ll transmute your contract vial into a suppository.

Sasha raises her glass.

SASHA

To next year.

Velvette raises hers.

VELVETTE

To quitting before some competitive lunatic owns your soul.

Lux raises hers.

LUX

To burning Lakewood if she tries again.

They clink glasses.

The television switches to an emergency announcement.

ANNOUNCER (V.O.)

Attention, Blackwood University.

The east stairwell has escaped probation.

All students are advised to avoid the athletic complex, the administrative
building, and any staircase displaying abandonment issues.

A distant SCREAM comes from outside.

Then another.

Nobody in the bar reacts.

Sasha drinks.

SASHA

Does everyone die this often?

VELVETTE

Freshmen mostly.

LUX

They’re biodegradable.

The Abyss doors burst open.

Yuki enters dragging a bloody freshman by the collar.

The freshman has a staircase railing through his torso.

FRESHMAN

I thought it looked lonely.

Yuki releases him.

He collapses.

YUKI

Three bourbons.

The bartender pours them.

One for Yuki.

One for the Reaper, who materializes beside her.

One for the dead freshman, whose ghost rises and takes the glass.

FRESHMAN GHOST

Am I expelled?

YUKI

Worse.

FRESHMAN GHOST

What’s worse?

GRIM REAPER

Your tuition payment cleared.

The ghost starts crying.

The bar erupts into laughter.

Outside, the Lakewood bus explodes.

Sugar Rush continues singing from inside the fire.

SUGAR RUSH (O.S.)

WE ARE THE CHAMPIONS—

A second explosion cuts her off.

Velvette leans into Lux.

Sasha rests her forehead against the table.

Yuki drinks like she is trying to erase the concept of employment.

The Grim Reaper checks tomorrow’s casualty schedule.

The bartender polishes a glass containing a screaming little storm.

On the television, the scoreboard’s face appears.

SCOREBOARD

This victory remains under protest.

The screen goes black.

ANNOUNCER (V.O.)

Good night, Blackwood University.

Final casualty count for today’s championship is twenty-eight dead, nine
resurrected, nineteen spiritually misplaced, one mechanically awakened, and two
declared academically ineligible.

Tomorrow’s classes will proceed as scheduled.

Have a productive night.

A beat.

The east stairwell crashes through the wall of the bar.

Its steps open like teeth.

Everyone looks at it.

VELVETTE

Oh, fuck off.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE 2
