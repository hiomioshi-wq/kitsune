BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY


EPISODE 13: “KEVIN IN SPACE”






TEASER


INT. MORTALITY SERVICES – MORNING


Kevin has been entrusted with one task.


A small cardboard box sits on the desk in front of him.


On the box:


ARCHIVED TRANSIT RECEIPTS.


TAKE TO BASEMENT STORAGE.


NOT TRAY THREE.


The Reaper stands beside him.


GRIM REAPER
What does the box say?


KEVIN
Basement storage.


GRIM REAPER
And what does it specifically not say?


KEVIN
Tray three.


GRIM REAPER
Excellent. You're ready for responsibility at the level of a mildly supervised shopping cart.


Kevin picks up the box.


Yuki hands him a clipboard.


YUKI
Get the receiving stamp. Bring the clipboard back. Don't trade it for a souvenir. Don't follow a familiar voice into a wall.


KEVIN
That happened once.


YUKI
You followed the wall twice.


Kevin considers disputing this.


Decides against it.


The printer emits a little warning chime.


PRINTER
Local reality experiencing duplicate addresses.


Yuki looks up.


YUKI
Define duplicate.


A second Yuki walks past the open door.


She wears a brass respirator and carries a harpoon.


Yuki stands.


YUKI
Excuse me?


The corridor is empty.


Kevin looks at her.


KEVIN
New uniform?


The office window fills with stars.


Not a view of stars.


Actual space, immediately on the other side of the glass.


A yellow school bus drifts past, covered in icicles.


Its driver pounds on the windshield.


GRIM REAPER
Oh, for fuck's sake.


The bus vanishes.


The ordinary basement light returns.


The printer spits out six versions of the same form.


One on parchment.


One on a punch card.


One as a wet clay tablet.


One on a novelty placemat advertising a normal suburban steakhouse.


Yuki lifts the placemat.


YUKI
Why is there a coupon?


PRINTER
That universe has sponsors.


The walls flex inward.


Kevin sways.


The Reaper catches the edge of the desk.


GRIM REAPER
Everybody away from reflective surfaces.


YUKI
Madison?


PRINTER
Containment remains sealed. Different fucking problem.


A circular gap opens beneath Kevin's feet.


Beyond it is a vast, starless white.


Kevin drops the box.


The box lands on the floor.


Kevin does not.


He falls through the gap, still holding Yuki's clipboard.


KEVIN
I DIDN'T TOUCH TRAY THREE!


Yuki grabs for him.


Her arm hits the edge of the gap like solid glass.


Kevin passes straight through it.


The Reaper reaches him for half a second.


Two fingers catch his sleeve.


Then the room stretches into a thousand rooms, and Kevin is gone.


INT. BETWEEN UNIVERSES – NO FIXED TIME


Kevin tumbles through black space.


Around him float fragments of worlds.


A diner booth.


A stone tower.


A section of campus fountain.


A vending machine wearing a crown.


Kevin sees the machine.


KEVIN
No. Not doing that again.


He kicks off its side.


The machine drifts away, offended.


A brass clock face appears ahead.


Its hands spin backward.


A woman's voice shouts from somewhere beyond it.


WOMAN
Catch the fucking rail!


Kevin looks for a rail.


Finds three.


Chooses the wrong one.


It turns out to be a sword.


KEVIN
Sorry!


A gloved hand catches his collar.


The clock face opens.


Kevin is pulled through.


CUT TO TITLE.


KEVIN IN SPACE.


The word SPACE is immediately replaced by OTHER ADMINISTRATIVE LOCATIONS.


Then replaced by SPACE again because it fits better.


ACT ONE


UNIVERSE 14-B — 1913


THE SILENT SERIAL


EXT. NEW BLACKWOOD AERODROME – NIGHT


Black and white.


A boxy frame. Scratches on the image. A piano playing far too quickly for the situation.


Airships hang above a city of gothic towers, smokestacks, and enormous clock faces.


Kevin lands on a metal catwalk beside a woman in a long dark coat.


Yuki's face.


Different eyes. Sharper posture. A brass prosthetic left hand ticking softly at the knuckles.


This is STEAMPUNK YUKI.


She hauls a harpoon line out of the disappearing clock portal.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
If you're about to faint, do it against the railing. I just polished the fucking deck.


No sound comes out.


An intertitle appears:


IF YOU ARE ABOUT TO FAINT, KINDLY LEAN AGAINST THE RAILING.


Steampunk Yuki stares at it.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Don't clean up my dialogue, you paternalistic little bastard.


A new intertitle appears:


SHE OBJECTS.


Kevin raises a hand.


KEVIN
Why can't I hear you?


INTERTITLE:


THE BOY HAS QUESTIONS.


Steampunk Yuki opens a panel on her brass wrist.


Turns a little key.


The piano stutters.


Sound floods the scene.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Borrowed audio. We've got twenty minutes before the organist complains.


Kevin touches his own throat.


KEVIN
Yuki?


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Depends who owes her money.


KEVIN
You work in Mortality Services.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
I work in a dirigible with six outstanding warrants and a boiler that hates Italians. Different fucking woman.


She studies his translucent hands.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
You're dead.


KEVIN
I know.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Good. Saves a difficult conversation.


Below the catwalk, the city flickers.


A neon mall briefly replaces a cathedral.


A horse becomes a motorcycle, then a horse again, then a very upset man in a horse costume.


KEVIN
Is that normal here?


STEAMPUNK YUKI
The costume? Unfortunately. The other two, no.


She leads him toward an airship cabin.


INT. AIRSHIP “THE UNREASONABLE” – CONTINUOUS


Everything is brass, wood, leather, and spiteful mechanical ingenuity.


A calendar reads 1913.


Kevin looks at it.


KEVIN
You're older than my Yuki.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Born in 1888. Started traveling in 1912. Spent nine personal years in places that hadn't happened yet. Do the arithmetic if it comforts you.


Kevin starts counting.


Stops.


KEVIN
I'll believe you.


She places a brass instrument on the table.


A ring of glass tubes surrounds a little star map.


The map is wrong.


Several stars occupy the same space.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Worlds are colliding. Whole histories are trying to share an address. My engine can find the seams, but it can't cross them while they're compressed like this.


KEVIN
But I did.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
You're dead. Living bodies insist on belonging to the world that made them. Yours is more of a strongly worded suggestion.


Kevin looks at his hands.


KEVIN
So I'm good at falling through holes.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Apparently, yes.


KEVIN
That's usually been a problem.


She clips a brass ring around his wrist.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Transit loop. Your status holds the crossing open. The loop lets living passengers borrow that status while they're attached to it. Nobody gets to let go until the landing.


KEVIN
Will it kill them?


STEAMPUNK YUKI
No. It tells the seam we're cargo. Don't ask the seam questions. It's already having a fucking day.


The cabin door bursts open.


Two clockwork security officers enter.


Their breastplates bear the seal of BLACKWOOD ACADEMY.


Between them waddles a brass goose.


CLOCKWORK OFFICER
Yuki Tanaka. Temporal theft. Unauthorized century use. Failure to return a library book in a linear fashion.


The goose hisses steam.


Kevin takes one step back.


KEVIN
We have a goose too.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
They travel well.


She fires her harpoon into an overhead beam.


Swings across the cabin.


Kicks the first officer into the second.


The goose opens its mouth.


A tiny cannon extends.


Kevin instinctively lifts the clipboard.


The cannon fires.


The little brass projectile passes through Kevin's ghost chest, strikes the wall, and pins a tax notice to the wood.


CLOCKWORK GOOSE
PAY.


Kevin looks at the clipboard.


At the tax notice.


At a stamped instruction on the officers' breastplates.


KEVIN
Wait. It says they need a receiving signature before they can seize property.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
We're rather occupied.


Kevin holds out the clipboard.


KEVIN
Who signs for the goose?


Both officers stop.


They look at the goose.


The goose looks at them.


CLOCKWORK OFFICER
The goose is an independent contractor.


KEVIN
Then it needs a different form.


The officers begin arguing.


Steampunk Yuki catches Kevin's arm.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Useful little bastard.


She opens the clock engine.


A seam appears beside the table.


Kevin steps toward it.


The brass loop expands around them both.


Behind them, an intertitle appears:


OUR HEROES ESCAPE THROUGH A LOOPHOLE.


Kevin points at it.


KEVIN
That one's fair.


They jump.


INT. BETWEEN UNIVERSES – CONTINUOUS


The two travelers float inside a fragile brass outline.


Worlds slide past like windows on an impossible train.


A huge white tear hangs far away.


Around it, entire cities are beginning to bend.


At its center is a face projected larger than a moon.


Kevin's face.


Smiling with immense, unearned confidence.


PROJECTED KEVIN
Attention, multiverse. Please remain calm. I have done the science.


Regular Kevin stares.


KEVIN
Oh, no.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
You know him?


KEVIN
I know the face I make before something bad happens.


The projection gives a thumbs-up.


PROJECTED KEVIN
A solution is already solutionizing.


Kevin closes his eyes.


KEVIN
That's a worse version of the face.


ACT TWO


UNIVERSE 52-H — 1957


THE ATOMIC SPACE MUSICAL


EXT. HEAVENLY ORBITAL COLLEGE – DAY


Technicolor explodes across the screen.


Silver rockets. Pastel domes. Students with enormous hair carrying textbooks about nuclear etiquette.


A chorus sings as they polish a satellite with feather dusters.


SPACE CHORUS
A brighter tomorrow!
A cleaner new age!
Please leave your mutations
At the edge of the stage!


Kevin and Steampunk Yuki land beside a fountain filled with sparkling blue coolant.


Kevin looks at the sky.


Three different moons overlap.


One has a shopping mall on it.


A woman descends through a column of golden light.


Velvette's face.


White wings. Gold chains. Immaculate armor. A radiant halo made of interlocking barbs.


ANGEL VELVETTE smiles with the patient joy of somebody who has invented a terrible treatment and cannot wait to administer it.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Lost souls. How fortunate.


KEVIN
Velvette?


ANGEL VELVETTE
By birth and divine appointment.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Don't let the smile confuse you.


ANGEL VELVETTE
I heard that, dear.


She gestures toward a cheerful sign.


REDEMPTION THROUGH ADVERSITY.


CONSENT OPTIONAL. PROGRESS MANDATORY.


Kevin reads it twice.


KEVIN
That seems bad.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Only at first. Then it becomes formative.


A student approaches carrying a broken model rocket.


STUDENT
I failed the test.


Angel Velvette lays a gentle hand on his shoulder.


ANGEL VELVETTE
And now you may learn humility.


She opens a little gold door in the air.


Beyond it, an endless corridor of disappointed teachers waits under harsh lights.


Kevin grabs the student's elbow and pulls him back.


KEVIN
Or he could retake the test.


Angel Velvette looks surprised.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Without a journey?


STEAMPUNK YUKI
He can walk to the classroom. Fucking journey.


The gold door snaps shut.


Angel Velvette's smile remains.


ANGEL VELVETTE
You are both very resistant to growth.


A distant collision shakes the campus.


The coolant fountain briefly becomes the east stairwell from Kevin's university.


Its steps open like teeth.


EAST STAIRWELL
Am I still on probation here?


Then it vanishes.


Kevin points at the sky.


KEVIN
That is the problem we're trying to fix.


Angel Velvette looks up.


Her halo turns like a measuring instrument.


ANGEL VELVETTE
The worlds are touching. Thousands will suffer.


A beat.


ANGEL VELVETTE
There is considerable educational potential.


Steampunk Yuki draws her harpoon.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Finish the thought carefully.


ANGEL VELVETTE
But suffering without supervision is wasteful. I shall accompany you.


KEVIN
Are you an angel who became like this, or—


ANGEL VELVETTE
I was born an angel. The wings are mine. The vocation is mine. No tragic fall. No mortal origin. I simply believe people improve when sufficiently devastated.


Kevin stares at her.


KEVIN
My Reaper would hate you.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Wonderful. Resistance gives us somewhere to begin.


A nearby launch gantry gives a sharp alarm.


Students gather behind a painted white line. A silver rocket rises from its cradle, shuddering beneath the overlapping moons.


Through its little window, Kevin sees three cadets strapped into seats.


A command board reads CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Oh. Today's resilience module.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Please tell me the module isn't the rocket.


ANGEL VELVETTE
The rocket is simply the learning environment.


A woman in a white technician's coat steps from the control booth.


LUX, this world's orbital safety director. Platinum hair, diamond pen, and the cold composure of somebody who has had enough meetings with Heaven.


ORBITAL LUX
Velvette. The exercise is canceled. The orbital lanes are overlapping.


ANGEL VELVETTE
An unexpected obstacle can deepen the experience.


ORBITAL LUX
An unexpected obstacle can become a fucking moon through the windscreen.


The rocket's engine begins to glow.


Inside, one cadet starts crying.


Kevin steps toward the gantry.


KEVIN
Do they know the launch is canceled?


ORBITAL LUX
The instructor's lock is still active.


She looks at Angel Velvette.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Their willingness to proceed matters more than the exercise itself.


KEVIN
They're strapped in.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Commitment needs support.


Steampunk Yuki moves toward the control board.


A gold force field stops her hand.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Only enrolled participants may interrupt the module.


Kevin looks at the failed-test student still beside him.


The boy is wearing a numbered class badge.


Kevin remembers the little tickets in the Mortality Services waiting room. How people hold them when they think the number means someone is responsible for them.


KEVIN
Can you end it?


STUDENT
I already failed. She says withdrawing twice establishes a pattern.


KEVIN
That's okay. We can have a pattern.


The boy shakes his head.


Kevin doesn't pull him toward the controls.


Instead, Kevin looks down at the gantry's material field. At his ghost hand. At the small service handle behind the shield.


KEVIN
Does the rocket still have an ordinary door?


Orbital Lux follows his gaze.


ORBITAL LUX
Yes. Emergency release on the outside housing. That's mechanical.


Kevin steps through the gold field.


Angel Velvette's smile finally falters.


ANGEL VELVETTE
You haven't enrolled.


KEVIN
I'm auditing.


He reaches the rocket.


Looks at two handles.


One says RELEASE.


The other says RELEASE, BUT NOT THE DOOR.


Kevin studies them both for an unusually long time.


Then chooses the first.


The hatch opens.


Orbital Lux cuts the ordinary engine feed from her console.


Steampunk Yuki and the failed-test student help the cadets down.


The last cadet is still shaking.


Kevin offers a hand. She takes it without worrying about its translucency.


CADET
Do we fail?


KEVIN
I don't know. You're out of the rocket, though.


Above them, the mall-moon passes directly through the launch lane.


The empty rocket's nose briefly becomes a food-court sign.


Orbital Lux looks at Angel Velvette.


ORBITAL LUX
Put that in the assessment.


Angel Velvette watches the cadets being taken toward the infirmary.


Her certainty has not broken. She is already fitting the interruption into her philosophy.


ANGEL VELVETTE
He showed initiative under pressure. The module worked indirectly.


Steampunk Yuki stares at her.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
You could watch somebody escape a bear and congratulate the fucking bear.


Orbital Lux hands Kevin a little service tag.


ORBITAL LUX
Keep that. It'll identify the local pressure bridge when you need it. And if you're taking her with you, keep her away from anybody who says they're having a difficult day.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Darling. You make assistance sound sinister.


ORBITAL LUX
You keep supplying the evidence.


A cheerful band starts up at the far end of the quad because the schedule has not been told any of this is inappropriate.


Music swells from nowhere useful.


“IT'S FOR YOUR OWN GOOD”


ANGEL VELVETTE
A little loss will teach you
A little pain will mend
A lovely little catastrophe
Can introduce a friend


I have a room for grieving
I have a chair for shame
I have a thousand ways to help
And none of them feel tame


SPACE CHORUS
It's for your own good!
It's for your bright soul!
Please surrender your objections
To the woman in control!


Kevin looks around at the smiling students.


Some are clapping with bandaged hands.


He takes a slow step closer to Steampunk Yuki.


KEVIN
This is really familiar, and I hate the new colors.


ANGEL VELVETTE
When you're broken, you'll be grateful
When you're grateful, you'll be kind
When you're kind, you'll thank the process
For improving your small mind


STEAMPUNK YUKI
Or you'll just be fucking angry
With a bill you cannot pay


KEVIN
Could we maybe stop the worlds
Before you ruin someone's day?


The music cuts awkwardly.


Angel Velvette folds her wings.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Very well. Triage before transformation.


She detaches one golden link from her chain.


It floats between them, holding the edges of the next seam apart.


ANGEL VELVETTE
My halo can keep a breach stable. Your condition can cross it. Her machine can identify the landing. We are well suited.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
That's a logistical statement, not an invitation to fix us.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Naturally.


She says it much too quickly.


Kevin keeps the failed-test student behind him until the gold door has fully disappeared.


Then he offers the transit loop.


They jump.


ACT THREE


UNIVERSE 78-C — 1978


THE PARANOID CONSPIRACY THRILLER


EXT. BLACKWOOD FINANCIAL ANNEX – RAINY AFTERNOON


The image turns grainy.


Long lenses watch from parked cars. Everybody wears brown. Every telephone sounds like it is being recorded by somebody with a pension.


Kevin, Steampunk Yuki, and Angel Velvette stand outside a concrete office building.


A newspaper blows against Kevin's legs.


He catches it.


CASINO DENIES PROMOTION EXISTS.


Below the headline, gamblers carry chairs out of a building.


KEVIN
We've had that one.


A man in a tan coat approaches.


MAN
You looking for Vale?


Kevin looks at his companions.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
We are now.


The man gives them a key.


MAN
Third floor. Don't use the lift.


He walks away.


Kevin looks at the lift through the glass doors.


A sign reads OUT OF ORDER.


KEVIN
I thought that was going to be more sinister.


INT. SASHA VALE'S OFFICE – LATER


Wood paneling. Filing cabinets. Cigarette smoke from an ashtray beside a visitor's chair.


Behind the desk sits Sasha Vale.


Sasha Vale is a cheeseburger.


A literal cheeseburger.


Two buns. Beef patty. Cheese. Lettuce. Pickle. A modest amount of sauce.


No human face. No little arms. No woman in themed clothing.


A cheeseburger on a plate behind a desk.


Everybody else in the office is an ordinary human being. The desk is a desk. The telephone is a telephone. The building is not made of bread.


Kevin stops walking.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Close the door.


Kevin closes it.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Sit.


Kevin sits.


He cannot stop looking.


A human secretary enters with a folder.


SECRETARY
Your four o'clock canceled, Sasha. Something about the sky splitting.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Tell him I still charge.


SECRETARY
Of course.


The secretary places the folder beside the plate and leaves without a second glance.


Kevin opens his mouth.


Steampunk Yuki kicks his ankle under the desk.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
You're the dead one.


KEVIN
Yes.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Good. The others have been expensive.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Which others?


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Men with his face. Some clever. Most exhausting. One wants to build a transit aperture inside our observatory and insists that the word inside is a technicality.


She indicates the folder. Her voice comes from the burger. The burger does not acquire a face to help.


Steampunk Yuki opens the file for her.


Inside is a glossy proposal.


UNIVERSAL RELOCATION THROUGH ADVANCED HOLE STRATEGY.


Prepared by DR. KEVIN, THINKING DEPARTMENT.


Kevin reads the first page.


KEVIN
Is Thinking Department a real department?


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
He wrote it on a door. That's apparently the accreditation process where he's from.


The next page shows a black circle, an arrow, a white circle, and a smiley face.


KEVIN
That's the whole diagram?


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
The arrow was added after questions.


Steampunk Yuki examines the proposed coordinates.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
His exit aperture is inside an inhabited universe.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Mine, initially. I refused the site agreement. He changed the host address. The seam survey still has to pass through here.


ANGEL VELVETTE
What did he offer you?


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Exposure.


A long silence.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
I sent him an invoice for wasting the word.


Kevin finds a page headed FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.


QUESTION: WHERE WILL THE DISPLACED MATTER GO?


ANSWER: FORWARD.


Kevin slowly lowers the paper.


KEVIN
Oh.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Exactly.


A car idles outside the window.


The camera watches it for too long.


A man lifts a camera inside it.


Angel Velvette opens one wing, blocking the shot.


ANGEL VELVETTE
Would you like me to introduce him to the consequences of surveillance?


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
That's my investigator. He's terrible at being discreet. Leave him alone.


Kevin looks at the plate again.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Do you have a question about the proposal or are we going to spend the afternoon doing whatever that expression is?


KEVIN
No. Sorry. Proposal.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Excellent. Look under routing.


He finds an empty envelope labeled ROUTE SEQUENCE.


KEVIN
It's empty.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Yes. The casino retained the cards as collateral against a debt it invented when I refused the site agreement. I hoped your arrival meant you'd brought them.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
We can retrieve them.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Then we can stop discussing the problem in my office.


She rings a small desk bell.


Her secretary enters.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
Get my transport tray. We're going to the Verdant.


The secretary nods and lifts Sasha's plate onto an ordinary wheeled document stand.


Nobody reacts.


Kevin studies the empty envelope very hard.


EXT. THE VERDANT JACKPOT – 1978 – EVENING


Rain streaks the casino's green awning.


A row of cars waits outside. Men in narrow ties smoke beneath a sign advertising GUARANTEED CUSTOMER DELIGHT.


A customer storms out holding a small porcelain frog.


CUSTOMER
They said it was the same as cash.


Kevin looks at the frog.


KEVIN
It isn't.


CUSTOMER
You work here?


KEVIN
No. I've heard the story.


Near the entrance, a broad-shouldered television reporter poses beside a camera crew.


CAPTAIN HERO.


Same ridiculous confidence. Different decade. A white suit, a red tie, and hair requiring its own zoning permit.


CAPTAIN HERO
Captain Hero Investigates. Tonight: is there something wrong at the casino, and can we keep the camera on my good side while it happens?


His cameraman lowers the lens toward Cheeseburger Sasha's document stand.


CAPTAIN HERO
Get Ms. Vale's statement. She's the actual source.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
The casino accepted a fraudulent asset agreement from a man who thinks a black circle counts as engineering.


CAPTAIN HERO
Powerful. Could you say that again while I'm nodding?


Angel Velvette opens her wings behind him.


Captain Hero sees her reflected in the casino doors.


CAPTAIN HERO
Oh. That's a spectacular visual. Are you available for the weather segment?


ANGEL VELVETTE
Are you available to confront your emptiness?


CAPTAIN HERO
Only if it tests well with the audience.


Steampunk Yuki pushes the doors open.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
We don't have time to watch a personality marry a camera.


INT. VERDANT JACKPOT – EXECUTIVE FLOOR – CONTINUOUS


Heavy carpet muffles the footsteps.


Portraits of important men pretending not to know one another line the walls.


Behind an enormous desk sits DEAN STERLING, younger and dressed as a financial consultant. His university tie is still visible beneath the waistcoat.


STERLING
Ms. Vale. This is an unexpected escalation.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
You retained my documents.


STERLING
Pending review.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
You've been reviewing them long enough to build a religion around the staple.


Sterling notices Kevin.


STERLING
Do you represent the project?


KEVIN
No.


STERLING
But you're Kevin.


KEVIN
Not that Kevin.


Sterling's expression becomes the concentrated terror of a man realizing his easy scheme has developed nouns.


STERLING
Our agreement lists a Kevin.


Kevin places his work ticket on the desk.


KEVIN
Your Kevin is alive. I'm dead. Different records.


Sterling looks at the ticket.


At Kevin's translucent fingers.


Then toward the locked cabinet behind him.


STEAMPUNK YUKI
That's where you've put them.


STERLING
Those are protected records.


Angel Velvette smiles.


ANGEL VELVETTE
I can help you experience the fear of losing everything you value.


CHEESEBURGER SASHA
That's already my part of the negotiation.


Kevin notices the cabinet's inventory sheet.


It lists the route cards as INTAKE MATERIAL, OWNER UNCONFIRMED.


Beside that is a reference to an asset pool.


He recognizes the format from the cascade of casino forms that once buried Mortality Services.


KEVIN
You filed them as abandoned property.


STERLING
Provisionally.


KEVIN
But you also say they're collateral. You can't release collateral without the owner's approval. You can't say there's no owner and then wait for the owner.


Sterling opens his mouth.

INT. ALTERNATE MORTALITY SERVICES – THE FIRST EVENING


Kevin is still at the half desk.


Madison's office light is on.


The alternate Reaper brings her a fresh mug of tea.


GRIM REAPER, ALTERNATE
I remembered the way you like it, Maddy.


MADISON
Of course you did, babe. You'd hate to disappoint me.


GRIM REAPER, ALTERNATE
I would hate it.


She says it with absolute sincerity.


Kevin watches from behind a stack of forms.


The gold boundary clasp stays warm in his pocket.


Madison leans back in her chair.


MADISON
New boy. Kevin. You still being normal out there?


Kevin lowers his head before looking toward her doorway.


KEVIN
Working on it.


MADISON
Aww. Growth mindset. We love that.


He stamps the next form.


His hand shakes only once.


MONTAGE — SIX WEEKS IN THE WRONG UNIVERSE


DAY THREE.


Kevin checks a public campus map. The streets have the same names. The town has the same river. The bus routes run to ordinary places inside the same universe.


None is a route home.


DAY SIX.


He visits this world's temporal studies office. Its equipment can move appointments through local time. It cannot reach a vanished interuniversal path. The lecturer charges him a consultation fee anyway.


DAY NINE.


He writes his original world's number on a piece of paper and places the brass compass over it.


The needle points inward.


Here.


Always here.


DAY TWELVE.


Madison gives him a pink employee card.


MADISON
Look at you. Practically permanent.


Kevin thanks her without touching the bowl of sweets.


DAY EIGHTEEN.


The alternate Reaper cancels her own break because Madison says she feels lonely.


She does it instantly.


Happily.


Kevin recognizes the spell's complete hold. He stops waiting for a sarcastic crack that never arrives.


DAY TWENTY-FOUR.


He hides his original clipboard beneath a loose drawer liner. The candy and the misspelled medal go beside it.


He does not throw away the ordinary little things people gave him.


DAY THIRTY-ONE.


An angry ghost starts shouting at the intake counter. Kevin reads the form, identifies the missing attachment, and sends it to the correct desk.


He gets it right the first time.


Madison notices.


That is not entirely good news.


DAY FORTY-TWO.


Kevin passes the desk without asking where anything goes.


He knows this office now.


He hates that.


INT. ALTERNATE MORTALITY SERVICES – NIGHT


The phone rings.


Kevin answers before the Reaper can turn from Madison's door.


KEVIN
Mortality Services. Please hold.


He listens.


On the desk, a new file opens itself.


VENDING MACHINE CASUALTY.


UNRESOLVED ARRIVAL.


Kevin looks at it.


For a terrible instant, he hopes.


Then reads the name.


Not anybody he knows.


Just another person having the same bad kind of day.


KEVIN
Yes. I know where that form goes.


He reaches for the right stamp.


Madison appears in the doorway.


MADISON
Bestie, you're getting so useful.


Kevin keeps his expression mild.


KEVIN
I have practice.


Madison smiles at the alternate Reaper.


MADISON
We should keep him.


GRIM REAPER, ALTERNATE
Forever, Maddy.


Kevin stamps the form.


The sound is small.


Final.


Outside the basement window, the stars are arranged almost exactly right.


Almost.


CUT TO BLACK.


END OF EPISODE four.
