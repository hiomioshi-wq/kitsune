EPISODE 3 — “CANON EVENT”

Premise

A relatively ordinary Blackwood student named BECCA QUILL discovers that the
antique typewriter she bought from the campus thrift store makes anything she
writes become reality.

Unfortunately, Becca is a catastrophically bad fanfiction writer.

She cannot spell.

She does not proofread.

She has no understanding of anatomy, pacing, geography, characterization,
continuity, or basic cause and effect.

And reality interprets exactly what she types.

This is not an apocalypse episode.

It is essentially a horrible Tuesday.
