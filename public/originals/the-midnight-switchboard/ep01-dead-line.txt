THE MIDNIGHT SWITCHBOARD
Episode 1: Dead Line

The switchboard on the fourth floor of the old telephone exchange only rang after 1:00 a.m., and only on copper lines that had been cut out of the city grid thirty years ago.

Nora plugged in the brass jack on Board 4. Static hissed through her headset, followed by the sound of someone breathing very slowly, as if they were underwater.

"Operator," she said. "Number, please."

"You're late," a woman's voice answered. "The train already left Platform Zero."

"Platform Zero was demolished in '98."

"Then explain why I'm standing on it."

The line clicked dead. On the pegboard in front of Nora, a red lamp flickered on beside Trunk 19—the line that ran straight down into the flooded cellar beneath the exchange.
