THE MIDNIGHT SWITCHBOARD
Episode 2: Wrong Number

By 2:14 a.m., Trunk 19 was warm to the touch.

Nora didn't pull the plug. When she leaned closer to the pegboard, she could hear a faint ticking inside the wood—not a clock, but the click of a rotary dial spinning backward, over and over, from nine to zero.

"Don't answer Trunk 19," the night supervisor had told her on her first shift. "If it rings, let it burn out."

Instead, Nora patched Trunk 19 into the main speaker. The room filled with the sound of rain hitting corrugated iron, and then a voice—her own voice, recorded twenty minutes in the future—saying:

"I'm already downstairs. Don't come looking for me."
