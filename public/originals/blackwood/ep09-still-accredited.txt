BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY
EPISODE 9 — “STILL ACCREDITED”

TEASER

INT. LUX AND VELVETTE’S PENTHOUSE — MORNING

A raven lands on the terrace railing.

It is wearing a tiny satchel.

It clears its throat.

RAVEN
Registered post.

LUX, in a white silk robe, takes the envelope from the satchel with two fingers.

Black wax seal. A crest: a skull wearing a mortarboard.

COUNCIL OF UNHOLY INSTITUTIONS.
QUINQUENNIAL ACCREDITATION REVIEW.

Lux reads.

Her teacup stops halfway to her mouth.

VELVETTE, still in bed, face-down, one arm hanging off the mattress holding a chain:

VELVETTE
Who died.

LUX
Nobody yet.

VELVETTE
Then go back to bed.

LUX
Accreditation visit.

VELVETTE
When?

LUX
Tomorrow.

Velvette lifts her head.

VELVETTE
Fuck off.

LUX
Nine a.m.

VELVETTE
FUCK OFF.

The raven clears its throat again.

RAVEN
There’s a second letter.

LUX
Of course there is.

She opens it.

Plain white envelope. Government franking. A cheerful logo with a tick inside a circle.

MORTAL HIGHER EDUCATION QUALITY AGENCY.
ROUTINE INSTITUTIONAL REVIEW.
TOMORROW, 9 A.M.

Lux puts both letters side by side on the breakfast tray.

Looks at them.

Looks at Velvette.

LUX
We’re double-accredited.

VELVETTE
Since when?

LUX
Since a dean in 1952 wanted a mortal research grant and didn’t tell anyone.

VELVETTE
Is he still alive?

LUX
He’s the east stairwell.

The raven hops along the railing.

RAVEN
Sign here, please.

Velvette throws a chain link at it.

The raven catches it in its beak.

RAVEN
Thank you. That’ll do as a signature.

CUT TO TITLE:

BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY
EPISODE 9 — “STILL ACCREDITED”

A rubber stamp slams the title card.

APPROVED.

A second stamp slams it.

PENDING.

ACT ONE

INT. ADMINISTRATION BUILDING — BOARDROOM — MORNING

An emergency meeting.

Around the table: Lux, Velvette, SASHA with three ring binders, POPPY with a pastry, MINA THREAD with a laptop and her cereal mug, the GRIM REAPER with a newspaper, YUKI TANAKA with an expression.

At the end of the table, a large gargoyle has been bolted into the wall so BLACKWOOD can attend.

LUX
Two inspectors. Same day. Same campus. One mortal, one unholy. Sasha.

SASHA
I’ve read both frameworks.

LUX
And?

SASHA
The mortal agency wants safety, student satisfaction, functional fire exits, and fewer than one death per thousand students per term.

POPPY
How many have we got?

The Reaper turns a page of her newspaper.

GRIM REAPER
This term or this week?

SASHA
The unholy council wants rigorous scholarship, sound governance, and a staff-to-curse ratio of at least one to four.

YUKI
We have that.

SASHA
We have one to nineteen.

YUKI
That’s better.

SASHA
That’s a lot more paperwork.

Lux taps the table.

LUX
Here is the plan. Tomorrow, Blackwood is a normal university.

Silence.

BLACKWOOD, through the gargoyle
No.

LUX
Darling.

BLACKWOOD
I am four hundred and twelve years old. I have been accredited by kings, popes, three revolutionary committees and a very determined goose. I am not pretending to be a fucking polytechnic.

LUX
Just for one day.

BLACKWOOD
The east stairwell has not missed a meal since 1952.

YUKI
He is the 1952 meal.

BLACKWOOD
He was delicious.

Lux turns to Yuki.

LUX
You’re our Compliance Officer.

YUKI
Since when?

LUX
Since this sentence.

YUKI
I work for Mortality Services.

LUX
And your manager has agreed to lend you.

The Reaper does not lower the newspaper.

GRIM REAPER

YUKI
You sold me for a quiet week?

GRIM REAPER
I sold you for a quiet week and a parking space.

YUKI
You don’t drive.

GRIM REAPER
I’m going to start.

Music begins. A bright, bouncy, aggressively inoffensive corporate jingle with something snarling underneath it.

“TOTALLY NORMAL UNIVERSITY”

LUX

Library books that never bite,
Students reading in the sunshine,
Nobody screaming in the night.

ALL, with fake enormous smiles

Totally normal, totally fine!

Nobody’s soul is on the line!

VELVETTE

LUX
Yes.

VELVETTE
Even the small ones?

LUX
Yes.

VELVETTE
Even Gerald?

LUX
Especially Gerald.

VELVETTE
I hate this fucking school.

ALL
We’re a totally normal university!
Clean, compliant, and correct!

That’s a reflection. Please reflect.

Make the death rate look like zero.

SASHA

MINA

SASHA

POPPY

YUKI

POPPY
That rules out most of the fun ones.

BLACKWOOD
I will not hide my stairwell.
I will not mute my halls.
I will not hold my hunger
For a man with clean overalls.

LUX
I will pay to fix your guttering.

A long pause.

BLACKWOOD
…All of it?

LUX
All of it.

BLACKWOOD, grudgingly
I am a totally normal university.

ALL
TOTALLY NORMAL! TOTALLY FINE!
TOMORROW WE’RE BORING
AND TODAY WE FUCKING SHINE!

The gargoyle sighs, and a little moss falls off it.

INT. EAST STAIRWELL — AFTERNOON

The stairwell breathes.

Yuki stands at the top with SAFFRON BELL, who has brought a trolley of food large enough to require planning permission.

SAFFRON
What does it eat?

YUKI
Mostly freshmen. Some faculty. A canoe once.

SAFFRON
I mean what does it like.

YUKI
Nobody’s ever fucking asked it.

Saffron rings her dinner bell.

DING.

The stairwell’s breathing changes.

Slows.

A warm amber glow appears deep in the stone.

SAFFRON
Hungry. Very hungry. Poor thing.

She rolls the trolley to the top step.

SAFFRON
Eat up, sweet pea.

The stairs ripple.

The trolley glides down them like a gift being accepted.

From the dark at the bottom, the sound of an enormous, polite, contented chewing.

Then: a long, slow, satisfied exhale.

Then: snoring.

YUKI
It’s asleep.

SAFFRON
It’s fed. It’ll sleep for a day. Two if I’d done the trifle.

YUKI
How long have you been able to do that?

SAFFRON
Since I was born.

YUKI
Why haven’t you done it before?

SAFFRON
Nobody asked.

Yuki writes on her clipboard: STAIRWELL. NAPPING. NOBODY FUCKING ASKED.

ACT TWO

EXT. BLACKWOOD QUAD — NEXT MORNING — 8:55 A.M.

The campus is transformed.

The gargoyles have been draped in tasteful cream fabric.

The vending machine that eats freshmen has a sign on it: OUT OF ORDER. An identical vending machine has been placed next to it, which is just a vending machine. Students eye it with deep suspicion.

The bleeding memory wall from the reunion has been painted magnolia. It is bleeding through the magnolia, slowly, in a way that could be mistaken for a feature.

A banner hangs over the gates:

BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY: A NORMAL PLACE.

Someone has crossed out NORMAL and written REGULAR.

Someone else has crossed out REGULAR and written NORMAL again.

Velvette stands beside the gates.

She is wearing a cardigan.

She looks as if the cardigan is wearing her and would like it to stop.

VELVETTE
I can feel my power leaving my body.

LUX
You look lovely.

VELVETTE
I look like a fucking librarian.

LUX
Librarians are terrifying.

VELVETTE
That’s true. The one on the third floor bit Kevin.

KEVIN approaches in a polo shirt with a badge reading STUDENT AMBASSADOR.

KEVIN
I’m the tour guide.

YUKI
You’re not a student.

KEVIN
I’m the most normal-looking person on campus.

YUKI
That’s fair, actually.

KEVIN
I’ve learned a script.

He opens his mouth.

KEVIN
“Welcome to Blackwood, where the futures of tomorrow are built today, yesterday.”

YUKI
Who wrote that?

KEVIN
Marketing.

YUKI
The chandelier’s gone.

KEVIN
They found his notes.

A car pulls up at the gates.

A small grey government saloon.

Out steps MS. GAIL PENDLETON. Fifties. Sensible shoes. Lanyard. Tote bag that says I ♥ QUALITY ASSURANCE. The friendliest face anyone has ever seen on a person carrying a clipboard that heavy.

PENDLETON
Good morning! I’m Gail, from the Agency. Don’t mind me. I’m just here to see you as you really are.

Lux smiles her best smile.

LUX
That’s exactly what we’re hoping you won’t do.

PENDLETON
Sorry?

LUX
Do! Exactly what we’re hoping you’ll do. Welcome.

Behind Pendleton, the sky goes dark for exactly one second.

A hearse pulls up behind the saloon.

It is black. It is very long. It has no driver. Its horn plays a funeral march.

Out of the back steps INSPECTOR AUGUSTINE HOLLOWAY.

A lich. Seven feet tall. Black academic robes, mortarboard, a monocle over an empty eye socket in which a small green flame burns. He carries a clipboard made of what is very obviously a tombstone.

HOLLOWAY
Council of Unholy Institutions. I am here to inspect the dread.

Pendleton turns.

She looks up at Holloway.

Holloway looks down at Pendleton.

PENDLETON
Hello! Are you with the drama department?

HOLLOWAY
I am with eternity.

PENDLETON
Oh, lovely. Is that a society?

Lux grabs Yuki’s arm.

LUX, through her teeth
They’re both early.

YUKI
They’re both here.

LUX
At the same time.

YUKI
I was given one clipboard.

INT. ADMINISTRATION BUILDING — CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

YUKI

PENDLETON

HOLLOWAY

YUKI

YUKI

KEVIN
Welcome to Blackwood, where the futures of tomorrow are built today, yesterday.

PENDLETON

INT. FACULTY OF MORTUARY SCIENCE — CONTINUOUS

Holloway walks the length of the room.

His flame-eye narrows.

HOLLOWAY

YUKI

HOLLOWAY

YUKI

YUKI

HOLLOWAY
Miss Tanaka. Do you know what the Council is accrediting?

YUKI
Scholarship. Governance. A staff-to-curse ratio of—

HOLLOWAY
Dread.

On it, carved in elegant gothic script, the rubric:

AMBIENT DREAD.
DANGEROUS ARCHITECTURE.
LETHALITY OF CURRICULUM.
QUALITY OF SCREAMING.
GOVERNANCE (SINISTER).

HOLLOWAY

YUKI
That’s good?

HOLLOWAY
For a dentist’s.

YUKI
Excuse me one moment.

VELVETTE

SASHA
Yes.

Velvette slowly takes off the cardigan.

VELVETTE
Oh, thank fuck.
