INT. BLACKWOOD EXAMINATION HALL — 8:58 A.M.

Three hundred desks in perfect rows.

“What is the purpose of an examination?”

He takes a breath.

RIGGS
“To find out what somebody knows. Not to find out whether they’re scared. Not to find out whether they can survive the room. Not to find out whether the paper likes them. If the student is too frightened to write, then the exam has measured the paper, not the student. So the purpose of an examination is to be fair, and anybody who isn’t being fair is not an examination. They’re just a piece of paper with opinions.”
