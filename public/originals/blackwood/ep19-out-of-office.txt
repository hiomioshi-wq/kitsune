SEASON TWO

TEASER

INT. MORTALITY SERVICES — MONDAY — 8:59 A.M.

YUKI TANAKA arrives with a coffee and the expression of someone who has already decided what her day is going to be, and it is quiet.

The GRIM REAPER is standing at Yuki’s desk.

This has never happened.

The Reaper does not stand at other people’s desks. Other people come to the Reaper’s desk. That is the entire organizational chart.

YUKI
What did I do.

GRIM REAPER
Nothing.

YUKI
What did Kevin do.

GRIM REAPER
Nothing.

YUKI
Then why are you at my fucking desk?

The Reaper places a form on the blotter.

ANNUAL LEAVE REQUEST.
DURATION: SEVEN DAYS.
DESTINATION: “A BEACH.”
APPROVED BY: SELF.

Yuki reads it.

Reads it again.

YUKI

GRIM REAPER

YUKI
Your mother.

GRIM REAPER

YUKI

GRIM REAPER
1880.

YUKI
Where?

GRIM REAPER
Krakatoa.

YUKI
That was a volcano.

GRIM REAPER
It was a working holiday.

The Reaper reaches behind her.

She lifts the scythe from its stand.

Seven feet of black wood and moon-pale steel. It hums very slightly, the way a fridge hums, if the fridge had ended civilizations.

She holds it out.

YUKI
No.

GRIM REAPER
Acting Death.

YUKI
No.

GRIM REAPER
Seven days.

YUKI

GRIM REAPER

YUKI

GRIM REAPER

She puts the scythe into Yuki’s hands.

It is heavier than it looks.

It is also, faintly, warm.

SCYTHE
Oh. A new one.

Yuki nearly drops it.

YUKI

GRIM REAPER

SCYTHE

YUKI

SCYTHE

The Reaper picks up a small suitcase.

It is black. It has a single luggage tag that says DEATH.

GRIM REAPER

YUKI
What if something goes wrong?

GRIM REAPER
I’ll have my phone.

YUKI
Will you answer it?

The Reaper pauses at the door.

GRIM REAPER

It is very long.

YUKI
Don’t.

PRINTER

YUKI

YUKI
