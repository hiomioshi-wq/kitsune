BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY
SPECIAL EPISODE: "THE DARWIN AWARDS"

INT. BLACKWOOD AUDITORIUM - NIGHT

A velvet-draped stage lit by chandeliers that hum at a frequency known to loosen fillings. A banner across the proscenium reads: BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY ANNUAL DARWIN AWARDS — "NATURE TRIED. WE DOCUMENTED IT."

Below the stage, Mortality Services has set up a folding table with a clipboard, three rubber stamps, and a bucket of ice for reattachments.

YUKI stands behind the lectern in an oversized blazer, rubbing her temples. LUX and VELVETTE sit in the front-row VIP box with flutes of champagne. KEVIN is in the third row wearing a tuxedo t-shirt and a bicycle helmet.

YUKI
Welcome, students, faculty, and survivors who technically owe us a co-pay. Because our accreditation board asked us to "demonstrate institutional self-awareness," Mortality Services is proud to present the first annual Blackwood Darwin Awards.

MADISON
(clapping enthusiastically from the front row)
Yay! Does everyone get a participation ribbon?

YUKI
Everyone gets a waiver, Madison. Some of them are laminated.

LUX
(raising her glass)
Remember, darlings: if you're going to die on campus, at least make it theatrical enough to justify the tuition increase.

YUKI
(checking her index cards)
Our first category: Best Use of a Warning Label as a Serving Suggestion. And the nominees are—

A spotlight snaps onto VIOLET CINDER, leaning against a marble pillar with a singed sleeve.

YUKI (CONT'D)
Violet Cinder, for attempting to flash-roast espresso beans using a containment-grade hellfire Bunsen burner in Dorm C's kitchenette.

VIOLET
It was a very dark roast. And the smoke alarm was already broken.

YUKI
Next nominee: Luna Voss, for testing whether a telekinetic shield can stop a vending machine that has already been kicked twice by War.

LUNA
The chips were stuck on B4. I stand by the math.

YUKI
And our final nominee in this category... Kevin, for—hold on, let me turn the page—for microwaving an unmarked thermos he found in the East Stairwell because, and I quote, "it was making a sad noise and looked cold."

KEVIN
It stopped making the noise!

DEATH
(calling from the Mortality Services table, without looking up from her ledger)
Because it achieved sentience and crawled into the vents, Kevin. We're still getting postcards from it.

YUKI
The envelope, please.

VELVETTE
(opening a black-edged envelope with a silver letter opener)
And the winner is... Kevin! Again!

Applause erupts. KEVIN stands up, bows deeply, and immediately trips over his own shoelaces, tumbling into the orchestra pit with a resonant *gong*.

KEVIN (O.S.)
I'm okay! The tuba caught me!

YUKI
(sighing, crossing a line off her clipboard)
Moving on to our second category: Most Creative Interpretation of "Do Not Feed the Architecture."

WAR
(standing up, cupping her hands)
That stairwell bit me first!

YUKI
You offered it a protein bar wrapped in barbed wire, War.

WAR
It looked skinny!

YUKI
And our final award of the night—the Lifetime Achievement in Still Being Here Somehow—goes to the entire third-floor chemistry wing, which has been legally classified as a weather system since midterms.

LUX
(standing, applauding lightly)
A triumph of spirit over common sense. Honestly, it brings a tear to the eye.

KEVIN climbs back onto the stage holding a dented brass tuba and a small plastic trophy shaped like a tombstone.

KEVIN
I'd like to thank the Academy, the East Stairwell, and whatever is living in the radiator in Dorm B!

DEATH
(snapping her ledger shut)
Ceremony adjourned. Nobody touch anything on the way out. Especially you, Kevin.

FADE OUT.
