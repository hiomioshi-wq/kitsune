BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY

EPISODE TWENTY: “THE OUTSIDE”

SEASON TWO FINALE

CONTENT WARNING: Strong language, extreme violence, grief, and suicide as a theme. This episode contains no method detail and does not make death look like an answer. If it gets too close, step away from it.

TEASER

EXT. BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY — THE RING ROAD — DAWN

There is a road around the campus.

Everybody knows it. Everybody has driven it, jogged it, been chased down it, been buried beside it. The Ring Road goes around Blackwood in a perfect circle, and past it, on every side, is a thick white morning fog that nobody has ever walked into.

Nobody has ever wanted to.

Nobody has ever been allowed to.

YUKI TANAKA stands at the edge of the Ring Road in her work cardigan, holding a coffee and the Reaper’s scythe.

She has not slept.

She is looking at the fog.

YUKI
(quietly)
Seven days.

The scythe hums in her hand like a fridge.

SCYTHE
Seven days is over.

YUKI
I know.

SCYTHE
She’s back.

YUKI
I know.

SCYTHE
So why are you at the wall?

Yuki looks down at her feet.

On the asphalt, in white paint, the way you would mark a crossing for children, a long white line runs the whole circle of the road. Under the paint, so worn you could miss it, someone has stencilled three words.

DO NOT CROSS.

YUKI
Because for four thousand collections I never once asked what’s out there.

SCYTHE
It isn’t your department.

YUKI
Exactly.

She puts down the coffee.

She steps over the line.

Nothing happens.

She steps again.

The fog closes behind her like a hand.

MAIN TITLES

ACT ONE

EXT. THE OUTSIDE — A ROAD — LATER

The fog does not last.

It thins, the way a lie thins when you walk far enough into it, and then it is gone, and Yuki stops.

She is standing at the top of a long hill.

Below her, as far as she can see, the world.

It is dead.

Not ruined. Not burning. Dead the way a room is dead after everyone has left and the last light has been switched off. A flat grey plain of ash and glass and collapsed motorway. Towers, snapped off halfway, standing like broken teeth. A river that has dried to a long cracked scar. Cars in lanes, rusted to the colour of old blood, doors open, nobody inside.

No birds.

No wind.

No sound at all.

The sky is the white of a television turned to nothing.

The sun is a pale coin somebody forgot to polish.

Yuki stands very still.

SCYTHE
(very quietly)
Oh.

YUKI
(a long beat)
Oh, fuck.

She walks down the hill.

The ash crunches under her shoes like old snow.

At the bottom she finds a petrol station.

Pumps leaning. Roof collapsed. A sign that must once have said something cheerful, now just a rusted frame. Against the wall of the shop, a row of plastic chairs, each one with a skeleton in it, dressed in the clothes of whatever year it ended. A woman in a sun hat. A man with a thermos. A little boy with a toy truck between his ribs.

They are all facing the same direction.

All of them facing the road.

Waiting for a bus that never came.

Yuki kneels in front of the boy.

YUKI
(her voice cracking)
How long?

SCYTHE
Longer than the university has existed.

YUKI
How long.

SCYTHE
It stopped before the first day of term.

YUKI
(not understanding)
Stopped.

SCYTHE
Everything.

Yuki stands.

She turns in a slow circle.

There is nothing.

Nothing at all, in any direction, to the edge of the white sky.

YUKI
Then where is everybody?

SCYTHE
You’re standing in them.

Yuki looks down.

The ash.

Grey, fine, soft.

She holds a handful.

It trickles through her fingers.

YUKI
(very small)
No.

SCYTHE
Seven billion.

YUKI
No.

SCYTHE
More, if you count the ones who were already dead.

Yuki wipes her hand slowly on her cardigan.

It leaves a long grey mark.

YUKI
Blackwood’s the last place.

SCYTHE
It’s the only place.

YUKI
It’s a fucking university.

SCYTHE
It’s a lifeboat.

Yuki laughs, a short horrible sound.

YUKI
With murder-football.

SCYTHE
Nobody said it was a good lifeboat.

BEHIND HER, a voice.

GRIM REAPER (O.S.)
Yuki.

Yuki closes her eyes.

YUKI
(not turning)
You said it was fog.

GRIM REAPER (O.S.)
I said it was out of bounds.

YUKI
(turning)
You said there was nothing out here.

The GRIM REAPER stands twenty yards up the hill.

She is not tanned any more.

She has no shell necklace.

She has her hood up and her face down and she is the colour of old paper. In her hand, no scythe. Her other hand hangs empty at her side.

GRIM REAPER
There isn’t.

YUKI
(gesturing at the plain)
THAT IS A LOT OF NOTHING.

GRIM REAPER
Come home.

YUKI
No.

GRIM REAPER
Please.

YUKI
(a flat laugh)
Oh, now it’s please.

GRIM REAPER
Yuki.

YUKI
You went to a beach. You came back with a necklace. You told me you were my friend. And the whole time, THIS was behind the wall.

GRIM REAPER
I can’t let you see it.

YUKI
I’m seeing it.

GRIM REAPER
I can’t let you know it.

YUKI
(stepping toward her)
I’m KNOWING it.

The Reaper takes one step.

The ash around her boots goes black.

GRIM REAPER
(very quietly)
Then I can’t let you leave.

ACT TWO

EXT. THE OUTSIDE — THE PETROL STATION — CONTINUOUS

The wind starts.

It comes from nowhere. It has no business existing, in a world with nothing left to move, but it comes anyway, a hard dry wail, lifting ash into long grey curtains between the two women.

Yuki raises the scythe.

It is heavier than it was. It does not hum. It is, for the first time, silent.

YUKI
(low)
What did you do?

GRIM REAPER
My job.

YUKI
Don’t.

GRIM REAPER
I kept the lights on.

YUKI
Don’t fucking lie to me, not now. What IS Blackwood?

The Reaper does not answer.

The wind rises.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(shouting over it)
WHO ARE THEY?

GRIM REAPER
They’re students.

YUKI
Are they alive?

Silence.

The wind drops, just for a second.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(very quietly)
Are they alive?

GRIM REAPER
(not looking at her)
Don’t ask me that.

YUKI
(her voice breaking)
Kevin.

GRIM REAPER
Yuki.

YUKI
Lux. Velvette. Sasha. The kid with the swan. The girls in the dorm. The ones who got shot in the football and got up again.

GRIM REAPER
STOP.

The Reaper’s voice splits the air.

The plastic chairs fly backwards.

The skeletons in them rattle and fall.

Yuki stands her ground, hair whipping across her face.

YUKI
You made me a receptionist at the end of the world.

GRIM REAPER
I gave you a job.

YUKI
You gave me a desk.

GRIM REAPER
I gave you a reason to get up in the morning.

YUKI
(a broken laugh)
I never got up. I never went home. I never ONCE went home, did I? I just… I was always there. When I stopped, when I slept, when I…

She stops.

The wind stops with her.

Her hand has gone to her chest.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(slowly)
I don’t remember my flat.

GRIM REAPER
Yuki.

YUKI
I don’t remember the street. I don’t remember a single morning that wasn’t that office.

GRIM REAPER
(very softly)
That’s normal.

YUKI
(whispering)
Is it?

GRIM REAPER
For the ones who come after.

YUKI
After what.

The Reaper says nothing.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(her eyes wide)
After WHAT.

The scythe in her hand wakes up.

It does not hum.

It speaks.

SCYTHE
(very quietly)
I’m sorry.

YUKI
(not looking at it)
Don’t you fucking dare.

SCYTHE
I can’t hold the file shut any more.

YUKI
Don’t.

SCYTHE
She’s lost the key.

The air in front of Yuki cracks.

Light pours out of it. White. Cold. The light of a hospital corridor at three in the morning.

Inside the light, a vision.

INT. A HOSPITAL ROOM — NIGHT (FLASHBACK)

A bed.

A young woman in it, twenty-six, Japanese-American, dark hair cut short by someone who didn’t know how. A tray of untouched food. A window, rain on the glass.

She is looking at the ceiling.

She is not crying.

She has run out of that.

A NURSE leans in at the door.

NURSE
Yuki? Your mum’s on the phone.

YUKI (FLASHBACK)
(a whisper)
Tell her I’m asleep.

NURSE
She’s been trying for three days.

YUKI (FLASHBACK)
Tell her I’m sleeping.

The nurse hesitates.

Goes.

The woman in the bed turns her face to the window.

She says, to nobody:

YUKI (FLASHBACK) (CONT’D)
I don’t want to be a problem any more.

The light in the room goes dim.

INT. THE OUTSIDE — THE PETROL STATION — CONTINUOUS

Yuki is on her knees.

The scythe is in the ash beside her.

Her hands are over her mouth.

Behind her, the Reaper has not moved. She has not come closer. She stands exactly where she was, hood down now, her face a quiet ruin.

She is younger than Yuki thought.

Nineteen, maybe.

Dark eyes. A scar through one eyebrow. A face that has been tired for a very long time.

GRIM REAPER
(gently)
That’s why you couldn’t remember your flat.

YUKI
(muffled)
Stop it.

GRIM REAPER
You didn’t go home.

YUKI
Stop.

GRIM REAPER
You didn’t get to.

YUKI
(a sob)
I’M NOT DEAD, I HAVE A DESK.

GRIM REAPER
(very softly)
Yuki.

YUKI
I have a coffee mug that says WORLD’S OKAYEST ASSISTANT.

GRIM REAPER
Kevin gave you that.

YUKI
I HAVE A FUCKING PLANT.

GRIM REAPER
It’s plastic.

Yuki lifts her head.

Her face is wet.

YUKI
(very quietly)
They all are, aren’t they?

GRIM REAPER
(a long beat)
Yes.

YUKI
Everyone at Blackwood.

GRIM REAPER
Yes.

YUKI
Kevin.

GRIM REAPER
Yes.

YUKI
Lux.

GRIM REAPER
Yes.

YUKI
(her voice rising)
THE GIRLS WHO GET MURDERED EVERY WEEK AND WAKE UP AGAIN.

GRIM REAPER
They’re not waking up again.

YUKI
(wild)
THEY’RE BACK AT BREAKFAST.

GRIM REAPER
They’re not coming back.

YUKI
(on her feet)
THEY ARE EATING CEREAL.

GRIM REAPER
They’re not dying again, Yuki.

The words land.

Nobody speaks.

The wind has gone.

The ash falls straight down like snow in a church.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(very quietly)
They’re already dead.

ACT THREE

EXT. THE OUTSIDE — THE PETROL STATION — CONTINUOUS

Yuki picks up the scythe.

She does it slowly.

Her hands are shaking so badly that she has to use both.

YUKI
Why.

GRIM REAPER
Yuki.

YUKI
(a whisper)
Why would you do that to them.

GRIM REAPER
(shaking her head)
It wasn’t me.

YUKI
Who then?

GRIM REAPER
I can’t tell you.

YUKI
WHO THEN?

GRIM REAPER
(cracking)
I CAN’T TELL YOU, I CAN’T, DON’T YOU UNDERSTAND? IF YOU KNOW, WE ARE ALL—

She stops.

Her hand has gone to her mouth.

Her eyes are enormous.

Behind her, the sky does something terrible.

A crack runs across it, left to right, thin as a hair, white on white.

The ground shivers.

The petrol station sign falls over.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(very quietly)
That’s what happens.

YUKI
(staring at the sky)
What’s that?

GRIM REAPER
It’s the seam.

YUKI
What seam?

GRIM REAPER
The seam. The one holding it all together. The thing that makes Blackwood a place and not a… not a…

She cannot find the word.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(whispering)
A grave.

Another crack.

Another.

Somewhere, very far off, a sound like a thousand windows breaking in a row.

YUKI
(stepping back)
You said if I knew, the world would fall apart.

GRIM REAPER
It will.

YUKI
You mean the university.

GRIM REAPER
I mean all of it. Every dorm. Every lecture hall. Every single one of them who’s sitting down to lunch this second thinking they’re twenty years old and have a midterm on Thursday.

YUKI
(very quietly)
That’s a lie. You’re keeping them in a lie.

GRIM REAPER
It’s a kindness.

YUKI
It’s a PRISON.

GRIM REAPER
It’s a second chance.

YUKI
(the word stopping her)
What?

The Reaper has dropped to her knees.

She is shaking.

GRIM REAPER
(a long, breaking breath)
That is what it is. That is what it was built to be. You think I wanted this? I was a… I was just a kid. I was someone’s kid.

YUKI
(carefully)
Reaper.

GRIM REAPER
Don’t call me that.

YUKI
What’s your name?

GRIM REAPER
(a pause)
It was Imogen.

She says it like she is touching a bruise.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
Imogen Vale. I was nineteen. It was nineteen eighty-seven. I was at a university very like this one, and I’d been quiet for a very long time. And nobody noticed. And one morning I decided that nobody would have to.

The wind stirs the ash around her knees.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(very quietly)
I woke up in a black coat. With a scythe. And a voice told me that I had been given work.

YUKI
(her face crumpling)
Imogen.

GRIM REAPER
Every Reaper was one. Every single one. Death isn’t a person. It’s a position. And the ones they give it to are the ones who left on their own.

YUKI
(whispering)
Why?

GRIM REAPER
Because we’re the only ones who know what it costs.

Silence.

The sky cracks again.

A long piece of the white falls away, like plaster off a ceiling, and behind it is nothing. Not black. Not dark. Just nothing, a place where the picture stops.

YUKI
(looking up)
That’s what’s outside the outside.

GRIM REAPER
That’s what’s outside the lifeboat.

YUKI
Who gave you the job?

GRIM REAPER
I can’t.

YUKI
Who.

GRIM REAPER
(shaking her head)
I can’t say it.

YUKI
Imogen.

GRIM REAPER
They sent me a letter. Every hundred years. Signed by the same people. The same sort of people. They never gave a name. Only a number.

YUKI
What number?

GRIM REAPER
(in a whisper)
A hundred and forty-four.

She says it very quietly.

The air itself seems to flinch.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(her eyes closing)
I don’t know what it means. I’ve never known. I only know that when the letter comes, I do what it says. And what it said, this time, was: do not let her cross.

YUKI
(realising)
Me.

GRIM REAPER
You.

YUKI
(a dry laugh)
I’m the one it didn’t want out.

GRIM REAPER
You’re the only one who asked.

She opens her eyes.

They are full of tears.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(very softly)
I’m so sorry, Yuki.

The Reaper stands.

The ash around her feet has gone completely black.

She holds out one empty hand.

A shadow gathers in it, long and thin and sharp.

It becomes, slowly, a second scythe.

Plainer than the first.

Older.

Made of nothing.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(her voice flat now, like a woman reading from a script)
Acting Death, you have exceeded your authority. Return to the campus immediately.

YUKI
(raising her own)
No.

GRIM REAPER
Return to the campus.

YUKI
No.

GRIM REAPER
(not unkindly)
Then I have to take you back.

She lifts the scythe.

The sky shatters.

ACT FOUR

EXT. THE OUTSIDE — THE PLAIN — CONTINUOUS

They fight.

It is not a duel.

It is two women in a dead world swinging the tools of their trade at each other in a storm of ash, and neither of them wants to win.

The Reaper strikes. Yuki blocks. The blades ring on each other with the sound of a funeral bell.

GRIM REAPER
(through her teeth)
Stand down.

YUKI
(striking)
Make me.

GRIM REAPER
(parrying)
I’m trying to keep you ALIVE.

YUKI
(a wild laugh)
I’M NOT ALIVE, IMOGEN.

GRIM REAPER
You’re HERE.

YUKI
(blow after blow)
YOU THINK THAT’S THE SAME THING?

The Reaper’s scythe catches Yuki across the ribs.

It does not cut her.

It sings.

Yuki staggers.

In the ringing of the blade, for one second, she hears voices.

Hundreds.

Thousands.

KEVIN (V.O.)
Yuki, please come home.

LUX (V.O.)
Don’t leave me alone with them.

MADISON (V.O.)
I’m scared of the dark.

A CHILD (V.O.)
I just wanted somebody to ask.

Yuki doubles over.

YUKI
(a ragged whisper)
They’re all in the blade.

GRIM REAPER
(white-faced)
They’re all in every blade.

YUKI
You’ve been carrying them.

GRIM REAPER
(raising the scythe again)
Somebody has to.

YUKI
(straightening)
Then let me.

She charges.

The Reaper parries, twists, brings the shaft round hard across Yuki’s jaw.

Yuki goes down in the ash.

Spits blood, which is black.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(on her hands and knees)
Imogen.

GRIM REAPER
(standing over her, shaking)
Stay down.

YUKI
I can’t.

GRIM REAPER
Please.

YUKI
(looking up)
Why aren’t you just killing me?

The Reaper’s grip tightens.

GRIM REAPER
Because you’re the first one in a hundred and fifty years who looked at me and saw a person.

YUKI
(very quietly)
That’s not a reason.

GRIM REAPER
(a sob)
It’s the only one I’ve got.

Yuki stares at her.

Slowly, she gets up.

She leaves her scythe in the ash.

She walks toward the Reaper.

Her hands are empty.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(backing up)
Don’t.

YUKI
(walking)
Imogen.

GRIM REAPER
Stop.

YUKI
(walking)
I know what you’re doing.

GRIM REAPER
STOP.

YUKI
You’re doing the same thing you did when you were nineteen.

The Reaper freezes.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(very gently)
You’re deciding for everybody.

GRIM REAPER
(a tiny, fierce whisper)
That’s not the same.

YUKI
You’re deciding they can’t take it. You’re deciding they’re better off not knowing. You’re deciding that if you lie to them long enough, they’ll be happy.

GRIM REAPER
They ARE happy.

YUKI
They’re eating cereal in a graveyard.

GRIM REAPER
THEY’RE SAFE.

YUKI
(softly)
So were you.

The Reaper’s mouth opens.

No sound comes out.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(close now, almost touching)
You were safe the day you stopped.

GRIM REAPER
(a whisper)
Yuki.

YUKI
Nobody asked you what you wanted.

GRIM REAPER
(her face breaking)
Nobody asked.

YUKI
I’m asking.

The Reaper’s scythe falls from her hand.

It does not clatter.

It simply stops existing, like a thought you forget.

She stands there, small and shaking, nineteen years old and very old.

GRIM REAPER
(in a voice like a child’s)
I want it to stop.

YUKI
(gently)
I know.

GRIM REAPER
I want it to STOP. I want the letters to stop. I want the names to stop. I want to go to a beach and not come back.

YUKI
Okay.

GRIM REAPER
I’m so tired.

YUKI
I know you are.

GRIM REAPER
(looking at her hands)
I can’t put it down.

YUKI
(very softly)
I can.

The Reaper looks up.

She understands.

GRIM REAPER
No.

YUKI
Imogen.

GRIM REAPER
(shaking her head)
That’s not what I meant.

YUKI
It’s what you asked for.

GRIM REAPER
I asked for you to stay.

YUKI
(tears running down her face)
I can’t.

The sky is tearing itself in half.

Pieces of it are coming down on the plain like falling glass, and in the gaps, the nothing, the end of the picture.

YUKI (CONT’D)
(shouting over it)
THE SEAM’S GOING.

GRIM REAPER
(frantic)
THEN GO BACK. GO BACK AND HOLD IT.

YUKI
(picking up her scythe)
YOU CAN’T HOLD IT ANY MORE.

GRIM REAPER
I CAN. I CAN, I’VE DONE IT FOR—

YUKI
FOR WHO?

GRIM REAPER
FOR THEM!

YUKI
FOR WHOSE ORDERS?

The Reaper’s mouth closes.

She turns her head.

Behind her, among the ruins, something is standing.

Tall. Thin. Robed in white. Faceless. On its chest, where a badge would go, a single burning number.

(very quietly)
That’s the Assignor.

YUKI
(her eyes on it)
It’s here.

GRIM REAPER
It never comes.

YUKI
It’s here now.

GRIM REAPER
(whispering)
Because you’re about to break it.

Reaper.

GRIM REAPER
(rigid)
Yes.

ASSIGNOR
Return the Acting Death to her desk.

GRIM REAPER

ASSIGNOR

I—

ACT FIVE

EXT. THE OUTSIDE — THE PLAIN — CONTINUOUS

The Assignor walks.

It does not hurry. It has never needed to. Every step turns the ash white, and every white patch spreads, slowly, like frost across a window, toward the two women standing in the ruin.

The Reaper is shaking.

She has her back to Yuki and her face to the thing in white, and for the first time in seven hundred years, or a hundred and fifty, or however long it has been, she looks exactly her age.

ASSIGNOR
Terminate her.

GRIM REAPER
(very quietly)
No.

ASSIGNOR
You will be reassigned.

GRIM REAPER
(lifting her chin)
I know.

ASSIGNOR
You will not be returned.

GRIM REAPER
(a breath)
I know.

YUKI
(low, urgent)
Imogen, don’t.

GRIM REAPER
(not turning)
Quiet, Yuki.

YUKI
It’s going to erase you.

GRIM REAPER
(a faint, awful smile)
It’s a bit late to start being afraid of that.

Ten feet.

Five.

She stops in front of it, a small black figure against the white, a girl in a coat three sizes too big.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(clear, steady)

ASSIGNOR
We made them a second chance.

GRIM REAPER
(shaking her head)

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)

Yuki.

YUKI

Imogen.

YUKI
(shaking her head)
No.

YUKI
I have the scythe.

GRIM REAPER
I mean mine.

She holds out her empty hand.

In it, a line of light.

It sharpens.

It forms, slowly, into the old black scythe, plain and heavy, the one she took off the stand in the Mortality Services lobby and held out to Yuki last Monday, and which Yuki, God help her, has been carrying ever since.

The second scythe, the nothing one, dissolves.

GRIM REAPER

She steps backwards.

Toward Yuki.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(softly, to Yuki)

YUKI

GRIM REAPER

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)

YUKI
(frozen)
What?

YUKI
No.

GRIM REAPER

YUKI
NO.

GRIM REAPER

Yuki.

YUKI
I’m not doing this. I’m not doing the thing that every single person in your life did to you. I’m not standing here and agreeing that you should go.

YUKI
It’s EXACTLY what you’re asking.

GRIM REAPER
It isn’t.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)

Then stay.

YUKI
That’s not true.

GRIM REAPER

She looks, for a long second, up at the cracking sky.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)

The salt-white ground has reached their boots.

(not turning)
In a moment.

Reaper.

(shouting)

She puts it on the shaft.

Her fingers are ice.

(her eyes meeting Yuki’s)

I’m so sorry.

GRIM REAPER
Don’t be.

I should have noticed.

GRIM REAPER

Not in time.

GRIM REAPER

She smiles.

It is the first real smile Yuki has ever seen on her face.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(very softly)
Tell my mother I took my shoes off.

Yuki closes her eyes.

She drives the blade in.

It is not clean.

It is not quick.

It is the worst thing she has ever done.

The Reaper makes a sound, a small, startled, human sound, the sound of someone being caught by something they didn’t expect, and her hands, clutching the shaft, are shaking, and then they are not.

She leans forward.

Her forehead rests against Yuki’s shoulder.

GRIM REAPER (CONT’D)
(a whisper, almost nothing)
Oh. It’s warm.

YUKI
(holding her)
I’ve got you.

YUKI
I’ve got you, Imogen.

Yes.

Why didn’t anyone say.

She goes still.

The black coat comes loose.

Underneath, a girl.

Nineteen years old.

Wearing a university hoodie, faded, three sizes too small, sleeves pulled over her hands.

Barefoot.

Yuki lowers her gently to the ground.

She lays her down on the white salt, and the salt, for a moment, turns green, and a handful of tiny flowers push up through it and open, one by one, small and blue and absolutely impossible.

Then the girl is gone.

Only the hoodie remains.

And in the ash, beside it, a pair of shoes.

Small. Scuffed. Neatly placed, side by side, the way you leave them on a beach.

I see.

I CAN FUCKING TRY.

ASSIGNOR
(very softly)
We are not your enemy.

(spitting ash)
You killed the world.

ASSIGNOR
No.

Neither.

Then what?

The Assignor lowers one long white hand.

A flash.

SOMEONE MUST ASK THEM WHAT THEY WANT.

(standing)

Where to?

Home.

ACT SEVEN

INT. BLACKWOOD — THE COMMON ROOM — NIGHT
