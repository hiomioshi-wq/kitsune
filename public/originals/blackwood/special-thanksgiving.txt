BLACKWOOD UNIVERSITY
THANKSGIVING SPECIAL: "MANDATORY GRATITUDE"

INT. BLACKWOOD DINING HALL - LATE AFTERNOON

A forty-foot mahogany table sits under three crystal chandeliers that were definitely stolen from an archduke. The entire Student Council, four Horsemen in civilian knitwear, and KEVIN (holding a foil-wrapped casserole dish that is leaking something faintly luminescent) are seated around a roast turkey the size of a Labrador.

LUX stands at the head of the table, swirling a goblet of dark cider. VELVETTE sits immediately to her right, already sharpening a carving knife with unnerving precision.

LUX
Welcome, beloved peers, hostages, and Kevin, to Blackwood's third annual Mandatory Gratitude Dinner.

KEVIN
I brought green bean casserole! Wait—no, I brought the pan I found under the radiator in Dorm B. Is that the same thing?

DEATH
(without looking up from her phone)
Do not open that foil, Kevin. There are three civilisations living in that Bechamel.

YUKI
(exhausted, clutching a mug of black coffee)
Can we just say what we're thankful for and eat? I have a three-hour shift at Mortality Services after this and the Reaper already threatened to dock my soul.

MADISON
(beaming, holding a tray of pumpkin-shaped cupcakes)
Oh, I'll go first! I'm thankful for *friendship*, and mandatory attendance policies, and the fact that nobody in this room has tried to leave the zip code since Tuesday!

WAR
I'm thankful nobody's set the tablecloth on fire yet. Give me five minutes.

FAMINE
(already halfway through a bowl of cranberry sauce with a soup ladle)
I'm thankful for carbs. Everything else is a distraction.

CONQUEST
I'm thankful for the seating chart. Look at how symmetrical it is. Look at how nobody can reach the exit without climbing over three deans.

VELVETTE
(setting the carving knife down with a soft *clink*)
Darling, you didn't invite the deans.

LUX
No. I invited the *concept* of institutional oversight, and then I locked it in the coatroom.

A heavy THUD echoes from the oak coatroom door across the hall.

COATROOM VOICE (O.S.)
Let me out! We still haven't reviewed the Q4 stairwell fatality metrics!

LUX
(raising her glass)
See? So much to be thankful for.

SUDDENLY, the roast turkey in the center of the table inhales.

Everyone freezes. Even Famine stops chewing.

The turkey's skin ripples. Two pale, human-like eyes open just above the wishbone.

TURKEY
(in a crisp, bureaucratic baritone)
Good evening. Before you carve, I'm required to inform you that this bird was sourced from an unaccredited dimension and is currently filing for asylum.

YUKI
...I'm going back to sleep.

KEVIN
Can I still eat the stuffing? It looks very polite.

WAR
(standing up, rolling up her sleeves)
Finally! A Thanksgiving where the food fights back!

DEATH
Sit down, War. If you cleave the table in half again, Lux is making us do dishes until Easter.

LUX
(smiling serenely)
Oh, don't worry, Death. The turkey can have asylum. As soon as it signs a meal-plan waiver.

VELVETTE
(pouring herself more wine)
Happy Thanksgiving, everyone. Pass the gravy before it unionizes.

FADE OUT.
