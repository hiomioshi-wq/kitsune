Kitsune Browser compatibility suite

Local pages that exercise one compatibility area each. Open them in Kitsune's browser by typing their path (for example /compat/rem.html) — same-app pages open in this tab now, so the engine can be tested end to end on this device — and compare with what a full browser shows.
